COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we speak

What to know before trading, sourcing or visiting the showroom.

Ask our team ↗
How do I start a gold or diamond trade?+

Tell us the product, quantity and destination you have in mind. Our Dubai team can discuss availability, documentation and the next steps before any trade is agreed.

What sourcing documentation can I request?+

Ask our team what origin, supplier and chain-of-custody documentation is available for the material you are considering. The documents will depend on the product and transaction.

Can I arrange a Dubai showroom visit?+

Yes. Contact our Dubai office to arrange a visit and confirm opening hours and product availability before travelling.

Is the displayed gold rate a trading quote?+

No. It is an indicative reference, not an offer to buy or sell. Contact us for a current quote, including any premiums, fees or applicable taxes.
